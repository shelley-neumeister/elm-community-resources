Why this exists

Technology decisions now move faster than most communities can follow them. The gap between how fast things change and how well the public can understand and respond is what we call the civic capacity gap. Awareness alone doesn't close it. What does is the practical ability to read a proposal, see who benefits and who bears the cost, and know where to speak up while the answer can still change.

Everything here is designed to be used together: in a classroom, a PTSA meeting, a library program, or a living room.
