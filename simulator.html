Civic Infrastructure Decision Game

Patuxent County

A data center wants to come to Patuxent County. It promises investment, tax revenue, and jobs, and it needs a lot of electricity, water, and public trust. Work together, ask questions, and decide what the county should do. There's no single right outcome. What matters is whether your group can explain its choice.
