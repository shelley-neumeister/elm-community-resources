Would You Rather?

Twenty choices about how AI shows up in everyday and public life. Each one sets two reasonable options against each other, and there are no right answers. The interesting part is what shaped your pick, and whether the person next to you chose differently.

Talk it through

  • What shaped your answer?
  • What would have to change for you to pick the other side?
  • Who in your community might answer differently, and why?

Your choices

Look back over your picks. Do you notice a pattern, such as always choosing speed, or always choosing the option with a person in the loop? Where did you break your own pattern, and why?

    Running this with a group

    In the room

    1. Read the card aloud. Give everyone ten seconds to decide silently.
    2. Have people move to the A or B side of the room, or raise hands. Switch to Count a room to tally on screen.
    3. Ask one person from each side to explain their pick before anyone debates.
    4. Invite anyone who wants to switch sides to say what moved them.

    Keep it moving

    • Five or six cards fill about 30 minutes of discussion.
    • Use the topic filters to match your group: School for a student club, Infrastructure for a community meeting.
    • A close split is a good sign. It means the tradeoff is real.
    • Close by asking which card was hardest, and why.
